## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **A sample-sign dial.** `sample_sign`, the first dial of Step 1, picks
  Restroom, Exit, Stairs or Room 101, each with its own wording and its Unified
  English Braille Grade 2 braille, lowercase with no capital sign, generated by
  liblouis with `scripts/generate_sample_braille.mjs` into
  `scripts/sample_signs.json`. The default stays `Type my own`. A picked sample
  prints a console note and shows SAMPLE SIGN IN USE in orange in the preview.
- **`braille_clearance_mm`** (default and minimum 9.5 mm, the ADA 703.3.2
  figure): auto-fit keeps the braille that far inside the border rails. With
  `auto_fit` off, a new warning, BRAILLE TOO CLOSE TO BORDER, reports braille
  that fits the plate but lacks the space.
- **Warnings in the preview only.** `show_warnings` (default `Yes`) draws each
  problem as red text beside the sign in the desktop preview, with OpenSCAD's
  background modifier, so it never reaches a render or an STL: NOT BRAILLE IN
  LINE n, TEXT TOO WIDE, TEXT TOO TALL, BRAILLE TOO WIDE, BRAILLE TOO TALL,
  BRAILLE TOO CLOSE TO BORDER, LETTERS UNDER 16 MM, and SAMPLE SIGN IN USE in
  orange.
- **Console notes** for `letter_height_mm` under 16 mm, `letter_raise_mm` under
  0.8 mm and `letter_line_spacing_pct` outside 135 to 170, each naming its ADA
  703.2 figure, and a `Text width estimate: {N} mm` line.
- **Checks that stop the render** when a braille dial is set outside ADA Table
  703.3.1, or `braille_clearance_mm` under 9.5 mm, from a preset or the command
  line, each with a message naming the dial and the figure.
- **A ready-to-print library** in `stl/`: both plates of the four samples as
  binary STLs, with `stl/README.md`, built by `scripts/build_release_stls.py`.
- **Documentation:** `docs/guides/quick-start.md`, `docs/guides/full-guide.md`,
  `docs/guides/maker-guide.md`, `docs/guides/user-guide.md`,
  `docs/guides/bom.md` and `docs/guides/design-rationale.md`, with preview
  pictures in `docs/images/` rendered by `scripts/render_doc_images.py`, and
  `okh.yml`, the Open Know-How manifest.
- **A documentation gate**, `scripts/check_docs.py`, run by
  `tests/test_docs_gate.py`.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_sample_signs.py`, `tests/test_ada_figures.py`,
  `tests/test_release_stls.py`, `tests/test_full_guide_covers_every_dial.py` and
  `tests/test_docs_gate.py`; new guards in `tests/test_source_guards.py` (the
  tab layout, dropdown labels, the braille sliders) and
  `tests/test_render_smoke.py` (warnings never exported).
- **Scripts:** `scripts/measure_glyph_advances.py`, which measures every
  character's width into `scripts/glyph_advances.json`, and the scripts named
  above, all listed in `scripts/README.md`.

### Changed

- **The Customizer reads top to bottom:** four Steps (`Step 1 - Pick a sample
  sign or type your own`, `Step 2 - Braille, pasted from a translator`,
  `Step 3 - What to export`, `Step 4 - Size`) and six Advanced tabs
  (`Advanced - Plates and border`, `Advanced - Raised letters, ADA 703.2`,
  `Advanced - Braille spacing, ADA 703.3`, `Advanced - Braille dot shape`,
  `Advanced - Braille plate lean and support fins`,
  `Advanced - Warnings and rendering`), with one plain sentence under each
  dial. The file's header walks through the four Steps.
- **Renamed dials.** A saved preset or a `-D` script must use the new names:
  OpenSCAD ignores an old name without a message and leaves that dial at its
  default.

  | Old name | New name |
  |---|---|
  | `sign_text_1` to `sign_text_6` | `text_line_1` to `text_line_6` |
  | `Line_1` to `Line_6` | `braille_line_1` to `braille_line_6` |
  | `char_height_mm` | `letter_height_mm` |
  | `line_spacing_pct` | `letter_line_spacing_pct` |
  | `letter_spacing` | `letter_spacing_factor` |
  | `cell_spacing` | `braille_cell_spacing_mm` |
  | `line_spacing` | `braille_line_spacing_mm` |
  | `dot_spacing` | `braille_dot_spacing_mm` |
  | `part_gap_mm` | `plate_gap_mm` |
  | `cone_dot_flat_hat` | `cone_dot_top_diameter` |
  | `cone_segments` | `dot_segments` |

- **Toggles say `Yes` or `No`:** `add_border` (was `yes`/`no`) and
  `support_fins` (was `On`/`Off`). An old preset's `yes` or `On` now counts as
  `No`, so it would lose its border or its fins.
- **`letter_height_mm` is the printed height of the capital I**, the letter ADA
  703.2.5 measures: the file passes the dial divided by 0.9555, Liberation
  Sans's cap ratio, to `text()`. To keep an old preset's print size, multiply
  its `char_height_mm` by 0.9555.
- **The width estimate uses a per-character table**, `GLYPH_METRICS`, measured
  from Liberation Sans, and gives the width a centered line needs.
- **The ten braille sliders** (the dot diameters, heights and cone top, and the
  three spacings) span ADA Table 703.3.1 and no further. `dot_shape` says Cone
  is not the ADA dot shape.
- **The default cell pitch is 6.5 mm** (was 7.0), in the file and both presets.
- **The console's summary line** prints millimeters to one decimal through
  `mm1()`.
- **The README** follows the Open Assistive Technology template's order.
- **CI's quick job** runs every test not marked `requires_openscad`, instead of
  two named files.

### Fixed

- **The capital I printed 15.29 mm tall at the 16 mm default**, under the ADA
  703.2.5 figure every document claimed; it now prints 16.000 mm.
- **Braille sat 4 mm from the border rail on multi-line signs**, where ADA
  703.3.2 asks 9.5 mm; it now sits 9.5 mm away.
- **The width estimate was off by up to 27 %:** eight W's printed 182.3 mm wide
  on a 160 mm plate, hanging past both ends with no warning. The estimate is now
  within 2 % and never narrower than the letters, so auto-fit widens the plate
  instead.
- **The braille dot sliders reached far outside ADA 703.3.1**, and nothing
  stopped a value there.
- **A braille line holding typed letters printed a plate with dots missing and
  said so only in the console;** the preview now shows NOT BRAILLE IN LINE n.
- **The summary line printed raw floats** such as 200.978.
- **The letter-height note and the documents said 15.9 mm;** ADA 703.2.5 prints
  "5/8 inch (16 mm)", and the note, the warning and the documents now say 16.
- **The README's parameter table named tabs that never existed, and its
  troubleshooting quoted warnings the file never prints;** both are gone, and
  the full guide lists every dial and message.
- **Three toggle vocabularies, an ADA comment attached to no dial, a
  three-line description of which the Customizer showed one line, and
  confusable names**, fixed by the reorder and the renames above.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-08-01

### Changed

- **Overflow warnings now report measured size against available space**, in the
  counted style the sibling cylinder generator uses for `TEXT TOO LONG: n/capacity`.
  The four warnings previously said only that something was too tall or too wide,
  which told you a problem existed but not how big it was or how much to change —
  so fixing it by hand meant guessing, rendering, and guessing again. They now
  read e.g. `WARNING: BRAILLE TOO WIDE: 242.1/156 mm (longest line is 35 cells).
  Turn on auto_fit, raise sign_width_mm to at least 246.1, or shorten the line.`
  The thresholds themselves are unchanged, so nothing warns that did not warn
  before; only the message is more useful. Geometry is untouched.
- Added a `mm1()` helper so millimetre figures print to one decimal instead of
  as raw floats like `47.32499999999999`, and hoisted the repeated
  `border_on ? border_width_mm : 0` inset into named `_inner_w` /
  `_letter_inner_h` / `_braille_inner_h` values shared by the checks and their
  messages — the warning can no longer describe a different limit than the one
  that triggered it.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-27

First release as a standalone repository.

**Commit history lives in
[braille-wedge-card-openscad](https://github.com/BrennenJohnston/braille-wedge-card-openscad),**
where this generator was introduced in v1.1.0 (2026-07-12) alongside the wedge
card and the charm. That repo now holds the wedge card only. This repository
starts with fresh history; nothing about the generator itself changed in the
move.

### Added

- **`Braille_Sign_STL_Generator.scad`** — two-part ADA-style tactile sign:
  raised-letter plate (Liberation Sans, uppercase, 16 mm, 0.8 mm raise) plus a
  braille plate (flat or 75° angled with break-away fins), a split raised border
  that joins into one continuous frame when the plates are mounted together,
  auto-fit sizing, and `sign_part` = Both / Letter plate / Braille plate.
- **`Braille_Sign_STL_Generator.json`** presets: Default Sign (both plates) and
  Braille Plate Only (flat).
- **Test suite and CI.** Customizer dropdown hygiene and preset/parameter
  consistency tests, source-invariant guards (single-file MakerWorld
  requirement, `sign_part` export options, matching letter/braille row pairs,
  pinned font), and OpenSCAD render smoke tests asserting watertight exports
  with the expected body count for the two-plate sign, the angled braille plate,
  and the letter plate. GitHub Actions runs lint + quick tests + render smoke on
  Ubuntu.
- **README** covering the desktop workflow, print guidance, troubleshooting,
  MakerWorld upload steps, and the ADA disclaimer.
- **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 LICENSE.**

### Changed (vs. the wedge card repo)

- The test suite is scoped to this one generator: `conftest.py` declares a single
  `.scad`, and the card-only guards (20 `Line_N` parameters, `_all_lines`
  wiring, preview-only warnings, the `text()`-outside-`warning_slot` guard, and
  the de-embossing mission guard) are gone. Sign-specific structural guards
  replace them.
- The `.scad` header records that this generator now lives in its own repo.
