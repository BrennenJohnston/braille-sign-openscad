# Sample sign STLs

Ready-to-print plates for the built-in sample signs, rendered from `Braille_Sign_STL_Generator.scad` at its defaults by `scripts/build_release_stls.py`. Each sign is two plates: print both and mount the letter plate above the braille plate.

- Letter plate: print it flat, letters up, at 0.2 mm layers.
- Braille plate: print it as modeled, leaning back on its fins, at 0.1 mm layers with no slicer supports, then snap the fins off.
